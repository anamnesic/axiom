# Counterexamples & Refutations — AXIOM Research

Registry of disproven claims, adversarial test cases, and concrete counterexamples discovered or verified by AXIOM.

## Structure of a Counterexample Entry

Every counterexample entry (`CTX-####.md`) must specify:
1. **Target Claim**: The refuted mathematical identity or equation.
2. **Identified Counterexample**: The concrete numerical or symbolic evaluation point demonstrating non-equivalence.
3. **Difference Magnitude** ($|\Delta| > 0$).
4. **Verification Method**: Fail-closed refutation (`status = disproven`, `guarantee = unverified`).
5. **Significance**: Why naive CAS systems or LLMs fail on this case, and how AXIOM's domain governance prevents false positives.
